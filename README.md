# Goblin Dungeon Janitor

A tiny, playable fantasy cleanup game. Click the dungeon mess to clean rooms, collect dropped gold, repair traps, shoo monsters, and buy upgrades before the next dungeon shift.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No install or build step is required.

## How to play

- Use **WASD** or the arrow keys to move around the grid, or click open floor to take a step.
- Get next to a mess, then click it (or face it with an arrow key) to clean it. Distant messes take movement to reach.
- Every step and chore costs shift actions. Monsters take two actions to shoo; a Trapwright kit makes trap repairs quicker.
- If you run out, choose overtime for eight actions, at the cost of that room's tidy bonus.
- Use the music button in the top bar to start or mute the original dungeon tune (audio starts only after you click).
- Pick which messes to tackle and route through cleared floor to stretch the shift.
- Earn gold by picking up loot and completing rooms; read the sealed notes to follow the missing mopkeeper's trail.
- Buy a Turbo Mop, Skeleton Crew, or Trapwright Kit from the janitor's closet.
- Clear all three rooms and choose one guild favor to prepare for the next, increasingly questionable dungeon.
